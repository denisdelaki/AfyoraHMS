Facility setup

From registration to your workspace.

Set up your clinic or hospital, then select the modules your team needs.

Step 1

Sign in or create an account

Existing users select Sign in. New facilities select Sign up to begin.

Step 2

Select your facility type

Choose Hospital or Clinic so Afyora can tailor the setup.

Step 3

Add facility details

Enter your facility name, address, contact information, and licence details.

Step 4

Verify your email

Enter the verification code sent to your email, then continue setup.

Step 5

Choose your modules

Enable the services your facility needs, such as Patients, EHR, Pharmacy, and Billing.

Step 6

Select a plan and launch

Choose a subscription plan, complete setup, then enter your dashboard.

About Afyora HMS

Who is Afyora HMS for?

Afyora HMS supports clinics and hospitals that need to coordinate patient registration, appointments, clinical records and facility administration instead of keeping these workflows in separate tools.

How does patient management connect to care?

Staff register a patient, find their profile and book appointments. Clinical teams use electronic health records for histories, consultation notes, prescriptions and test results. Access depends on the facility's enabled modules and staff permissions.

Which operational modules are available?

Pharmacy supports medicines and prescriptions; billing supports invoices, payments and balances. Inventory, laboratory, radiology, employee, department and reporting modules support the wider facility workflow.

How can a facility get started or raise a privacy question?

Create a facility account to begin setup. For the existing privacy contact and data-handling information, read the Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement.
