Visual walkthrough

Follow the setup, one screen at a time.

Use these screens as your guide from your first visit to a ready-to-use Afyora workspace.

Step 1

Sign in or create an account

Existing users select Sign in. New facilities select Sign up to begin.

Step 2

Select your facility type

Choose Hospital or Clinic so Afyora can tailor the setup.

Step 3

Add facility details

Enter your facility name, address, contact information, and licence details.

Step 4

Verify your email

Enter the verification code sent to your email, then continue setup.

Step 5

Choose your modules

Enable the services your facility needs, such as Patients, EHR, Pharmacy, and Billing.

Step 6

Select a plan and launch

Choose a subscription plan, complete setup, then enter your dashboard.

After sign-in

Start at the dashboard, then use the sidebar.

Your dashboard gives you an at-a-glance view of appointments, revenue, stock, and activity. Select an item in the left sidebar to open that workspace.
