1. Preamble, Roles & Definitions

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms a legally binding contract between the registering healthcare entity ("Healthcare Facility", "Hospital", "Clinic", or "Data Controller") and Afyora Technologies Ltd. ("Afyora HMS", "Provider", or "Data Processor").

This DPA applies to the processing of all Personal Data, Protected Health Information (PHI), Electronic Health Records (EHR), financial records, and operational information provided by or generated on behalf of the Healthcare Facility through its subscription to the Afyora HMS platform.

"Data Controller" means the Healthcare Facility that determines the purposes and lawful bases for processing patient health records and facility staff data.

"Data Processor" means Afyora Technologies Ltd., which processes Personal Data solely on behalf of, and under the documented instructions of, the Data Controller.

"Personal Data" & "Protected Health Information (PHI)" mean any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (patient, guardian, employee) collected or processed within Afyora HMS.

"Applicable Data Protection Law" refers to the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019), Data Protection (General) Regulations 2021, Digital Health Act 2023, HIPAA Privacy & Security Rules, GDPR principles, and relevant healthcare privacy regulations.

2. Absolute Data Ownership & Controller Instructions

Section 2.1 — Exclusive Ownership: The Healthcare Facility retains sole, absolute, and unalienable title, ownership, copyright, and intellectual property rights in all data uploaded, created, stored, or processed in Afyora HMS, including patient charts, diagnoses, KNHTS/ICD codes, lab results, prescriptions, invoices, and employee records ("Facility Data").

2.2 — Zero Provider Rights: Afyora HMS acquires no right, title, or interest in or to any Facility Data. Nothing in this DPA or the Terms of Service shall be construed as granting Afyora HMS any licence, right, or implied claim over Facility Data, except the limited right to host and process such data strictly for the performance of the services.

2.3 — Strict Written Instructions: Afyora HMS shall process Facility Data strictly and exclusively in accordance with the documented instructions of the Healthcare Facility (including instructions issued through system administrative settings). Afyora HMS shall never:

    Sell, rent, trade, or lease Facility Data or patient health records to any third party. Use Facility Data for marketing, advertising, profiling, or commercial data aggregation. Use Facility Data to train machine learning models without explicit written permission from the Healthcare Facility. Disclose Facility Data to any external entity except as explicitly required by law or judicial order (with immediate prior notice to the Healthcare Facility where legally permissible).

3. Scope & Categories of Data Processing

The subject-matter, duration, nature, and purpose of data processing under this Agreement are defined as follows:

Categories of Data Subjects

  • Patients, guardians, next-of-kin, and emergency contacts
  • Attending doctors, nurses, surgeons, and clinical specialists
  • Pharmacists, laboratory technicians, and radiologists
  • Facility administrators, billing clerks, and receptionists

Categories of Data

  • Demographics: Name, National ID/Passport, DOB, Phone, Email, Address
  • Clinical: Electronic Health Records, Vital signs, Medical history, KNHTS coded diagnoses, Prescriptions, Lab & Imaging results
  • Financial: Invoices, Insurance policy numbers, Claims, Payments
  • System: User activity logs, Role assignments, Audit trails

4. Security Safeguards & Technical Measures

Afyora HMS implements comprehensive technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the sensitive medical nature of Facility Data:

lock
Encryption Standards: All data transmitted over networks is encrypted using TLS 1.3 protocol. All sensitive database fields, file attachments, and backups are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standard encryption.
admin_panel_settings
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Access to clinical records is restricted based on user role assignments configured by the Healthcare Facility. Administrative logins require strong passwords and multi-factor authentication options.
history
Immutable Audit Trails: The system maintains an unalterable audit log recording every creation, view, update, export, or deletion of patient health records, including timestamp and user ID.
cloud_done
Backup & Resilience: Daily automated encrypted backups with multi-region redundancy, zero-data-loss database replication, and regular disaster recovery simulation testing.

5. Confidentiality & Personnel Security

Afyora HMS shall ensure that all employees, contractors, and technical staff authorized to access or assist with Facility Data are bound by strict contractual confidentiality agreements.

Provider staff access to production environment data is granted strictly on a "least-privilege" and "need-to-know" basis, reserved exclusively for troubleshooting authorized system issues under logged support tickets.

6. Sub-processors & Infrastructure Partners

The Healthcare Facility grants general authorization to Afyora HMS to engage tier-1 cloud infrastructure and utility sub-processors (such as cloud hosting providers, database providers, and SMS gateway services for patient notifications).

Afyora HMS confirms that all sub-processors are bound by written agreements providing data protection standards no less restrictive than those set out in this DPA. Afyora HMS shall notify the Healthcare Facility of any intended changes or additions to sub-processors at least 14 days in advance.

7. Data Subject Rights & Patient Requests

As Data Controller, the Healthcare Facility is responsible for responding to requests from patients or employees exercising their statutory rights (access, correction, erasure, portability, or restriction of processing).

Afyora HMS shall provide appropriate technical capabilities within the software (such as patient profile export, correction tools, and record archiving) to enable the Healthcare Facility to fulfill such data subject requests without delay.

8. Incident Management & 48-Hour Breach Notification

Mandatory Incident Response Protocol: In the event of any confirmed or reasonably suspected security breach, unauthorized access, or loss of Facility Data, Afyora HMS shall notify the Healthcare Facility in writing within 48 hours of discovery.

The notification shall contain: (a) description of the nature of the breach; (b) categories and approximate number of data records affected; (c) measures taken or proposed to mitigate potential adverse effects; and (d) contact details of the Data Protection Officer or Lead Technical Officer.

Afyora HMS shall immediately initiate containment and remediation measures at its own expense and assist the Healthcare Facility in notifying relevant data protection authorities where required by law.

9. Data Return, Export & Complete Deletion

9.1 — Full Data Export Guarantee: Upon termination or expiration of the Healthcare Facility's subscription, Afyora HMS shall provide the Facility with a full, unencumbered export of all Electronic Health Records, patient demographics, clinical encounter notes, lab results, prescriptions, and financial ledgers in standard machine-readable formats (JSON, CSV, FHIR-compliant archives) during a 60-day post-termination window.

9.2 — Permanent Deletion: Following the completion of data export or the expiration of the 60-day retrieval period, Afyora HMS shall permanently delete, sanitize, and overwrite all copies of Facility Data residing in production databases and backup systems in accordance with NIST SP 800-88 standards.

9.3 — Certificate of Destruction: Upon written request from the Healthcare Facility, Afyora HMS shall issue a signed Certificate of Data Destruction confirming that all active and backup copies of Facility Data have been irreversibly purged.

10. Audit Rights & Compliance Verification

Upon reasonable written request, Afyora HMS shall make available to the Healthcare Facility all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, including executive summaries of third-party security audits, ISO/SOC certifications, or vulnerability penetration testing reports.

11. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This DPA shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Kenya. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this DPA shall be submitted to the jurisdiction of the competent courts and data protection regulatory authorities in Kenya.

Electronic Execution & Legal Acceptance

By creating an account on Afyora HMS and selecting "I accept the Data Processing Agreement (DPA)" during signup, the registering individual certifies that they are an authorized officer or administrator of the Healthcare Facility and accept all terms of this DPA on behalf of the Facility.
