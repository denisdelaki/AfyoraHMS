Important Distinction: Patient Records vs. Platform Accounts

All Patient Health Information (PHI) and clinical facility records processed by Afyora HMS are strictly governed by our Data Processing Agreement (DPA), under which your Healthcare Facility is the sole Data Controller. This Privacy Policy describes only how we collect and manage account metadata for facility administrators, subscribers, and website visitors.

1. Information We Collect

We collect administrative contact and platform telemetry data necessary to operate the platform:

  • Facility & Admin Contact Details: Facility name, license registration number, administrator name, official email, phone number, and physical billing address provided during signup.
  • Authentication Credentials: Encrypted passwords and security tokens.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and system access timestamps logged for security monitoring.

2. How We Use Account Data

We use account information strictly to:

  • Provision, maintain, and secure your facility account workspace.
  • Verify facility licensing and administrator identity.
  • Send security alerts, billing invoices, and critical system notifications.
  • Provide customer support and technical troubleshooting.

3. Security & Compliance Safeguards

We employ industry-leading security practices, including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, strict role-based access control, and 24/7 infrastructure security monitoring compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and international health IT privacy standards.

4. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

For privacy inquiries, data protection compliance requests, or regulatory questions, contact our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@afyorahms.com.
